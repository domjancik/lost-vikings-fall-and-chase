# Eight-family native Viking motion

The accepted84 correction fixes visible trial height and gait cadence. It cannot
invent missing authored leg exchanges.85 addresses that source limitation.

## Source contract

Every output cell is authored directly at64×64 native pixels, one native pixel
per two world units; origin32,40 and floor sole-exclusive62. The established
horn-inclusive standing budgets are72/76/72 world units. The reviewed81 anatomy
is projected uniformly as geometry by0.925 before rasterization. This is not
bitmap resampling and does not claim anatomical36/38/36 after that projection.

The source is tools/native-vikings85-gaits.py and its imported retained native
pose rigs78–81. The original85 imagegen attempts and exact prompts are retained
in assets/vikings85. All three failed the source-density gate; they provide
material direction only and are never sampled or processed into game assets.

assets/vikings85-native contains eight near/far leg families per hero and full
32/38/60 pose kits. Whole bodies are rendered independently from the articulated
rig in every pose, including shoulders, armor, hands and beard motion. No fixed
torso rows are joined to independently generated legs. Binary alpha is authored
directly. The native output is the raw source and requires no grid-fixer pass.

Helmet, pauldron, chest, leather and shield planes use connected native clusters.
Olaf's round disc keeps its physical24-native diameter. Its raised projection
is centered on native22, the middle walking plane (world-36); shoulder shrug
and fixed-length arm IK preserve underside grip after geometry registration.

## Gate state

The first direct-native rendition passed density and actual opposing leg motion,
but failed material/style quality. It was revised with broader helmet dome,
rounded armor planes, articulated leather boot shafts and a wood/rim/boss shield.
Source and runtime verdicts remain governed by VIKINGS85-REVIEW.md. No runtime
integration or publication is authorized by technical dimensions alone.

## Integration and verification

The full source gate was accepted after the rear ladder helmet correction.
Native85 is a separate selectable skin, with the current, optional imagegen,
previous and original procedural choices retained. Each pose uses its new
full native kit, including round Olaf equipment; no legacy shield is doubled.
The accepted84 distance-based cadence remains unchanged.

Native85-registration.js exports a pure per-cell projected shield ellipse from
the exact source equipment geometry. The parent integrated it into production
skin binding and swept projectile collision, retaining numeric shieldPose.
The source registration helper is regenerated alongside the native assets.
