# Water room correction contract

The water83 machinery was replaced with simplified hand-authored art after its
generated sheets failed review. That fallback does not meet the Clockwork art
standard. Water87 replaces the devices with independently reviewed generated
native-density material, retaining original sources and rejected attempts.

The basin has solid retaining banks, with matching physical walls at x320 and
x2100 down to floor1000. The right ladder mounts on the wet face at x2080 and
joins a supported44-unit dock projection. Water may end against masonry; it
must never have a freestanding vertical edge in open space.

Olaf uses damped buoyancy, not a position assignment. Deep entry, release from
a submerged ladder, settling at the surface and following a draining surface
must all have continuous trajectories. Front-shield pushing meets the actual
42-unit shield edge, in both directions, only near the floating pontoon.

The crank uses one generated fixed housing, a turning asymmetric wheel and a
threaded rod that visibly travels with authoritative wound0..1. Partial progress
must persist after release, and unwind must reverse the same physical motion.
The valve wheel and service gate move together; the horizontal sluice opens
continuously before the water recedes. The intact retaining link must visibly
connect before cutting and separate after cutting. No progress-panel overlay
can replace changes in the machine's own pixels.

Native wheel rotations are rasterized at native resolution and enlarged on the
same two-world-unit grid as their fixed housings. Component ports come from the
final registered source. Bells require real transparent open-bottom cavities
matching the physical air volumes and enough clearance for adult Vikings.

Review requires raw/native material acceptance, then actual gameplay1x/2x and
mobile: valve before/moving/after, chain intact/cut, crank zero/half/complete,
sluice closed/opening/open, bell occupants, hose ports, retaining sides and
Olaf's ordinary-control ascent. Focused trajectory tests and complete full-health
60/120Hz and browser routes supplement these visual gates.

Swimming frames are deferred until the Viking art direction is settled.

The compressor lands on a generated wheeled cradle as the surface recedes.
Its shared deck stops at744, placing the actual underside against the800 beam.
The beam is a real one-way walking support; its columns and tires reach floor1000.
The raised crank rod clears the beam underside, the refuge stays open, and the
flexible hose routes around the frame rather than through its support bar.
The full248-unit carrier envelope, rather than just its deck, clears both banks.
