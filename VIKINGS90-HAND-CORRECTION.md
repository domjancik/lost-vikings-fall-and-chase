# Erik's walking hands

The far palm in walk phases 3, 4 and 5 occupied only one or two native flesh
pixels beside a dark cuff and beard. Phase 6 had excessive brown shadow on the
palm. They remained nominally skin in the source, but read as dark stubs at
gameplay size. This was silhouette/material readability, not a gray palette
introduced by processing.

Imagegen edited each complete original pose, preserving the body root, leg
trajectory, boots and near hand. Selected replacements are walk3/5/6 V5 and
walk4 V6; walk4 V5 was rejected because it added an upper fist while retaining
the lower hand. All originals and exact prompts remain in assets/vikings90.

The independent reviewer accepted the complete source/native row and then
fresh production evidence: three actual eight-frame loops, both zooms, mobile
and mirrored motion. The evidence board is
output/vikings90-review/erik-corrected-hands-actual-three-loops.png;
full verdicts are recorded in VIKINGS90-CYCLE-REVIEW.md.

Skin90 also receives the same registered shield ellipse as the reviewed
Imagegen88 art. Runtime assignment and projectile collision are covered by
hero-shield90.test.js. The game preserves procedural and previous skin options.

These two hero approvals do not approve unfinished Olaf art or publication of
the complete three-character skin. Frozen release90 inherits the verified
Warden91 release and overlays only the listed Viking implementation files.
