# Cycle of Depths — independent critique and validation

## Conceptual verdict before implementation

The causal sequence is promising and distinct from the existing Story campaign: a restrained circulation drive, a mechanically braked vent, a fragile damper catch, shield-assisted ascent and a dock-committed rail change. Common objects should respond to their material/action relationship, not a named hero. The gallery handle should work for any hero who reaches it; the draft's shield requirement is an intrinsic physical capability.

Read `CYCLE-OF-DEPTHS-SPEC.md`, current movement integration and shield geometry. This is a conceptual/code-compatibility review, not a simulated route or browser playthrough. No new implementation exists in the inspected files. The following unresolved details are blocking acceptance until made explicit and validated.

## Required corrections / explicit implementation contracts

1. **Recovery below the gaps is not yet specified.** Left floor ends at1120, staging starts1280 and ends1440, right floor starts1700. A missed cradle therefore falls into two unsupported shafts. Current shared movement defaults to fatal `deathY=700`, while this level's main floor is940. Set the level-specific death boundary below the complete world and add reachable lower catch basins with return ladders or continuously available transport. Merely removing instant-kill hazards does not make a pit recoverable. Verify every hero can independently return after a missed boarding; no restart may be required to restore an essential teammate.

2. **Specify all carrier landing geometry and lap commitment.** The lower rail needs explicit top500, bottom940 and left/right corner definitions, with carrier collision/render anchors agreeing at each dock. The bottom-left route commit must occur while fully docked, preserving position, riders, facing and accumulated movement. State how the empty cradle reaches an upper stranded hero after a selector reversal. A high-gallery selector must remain reachable after choosing low, so an accidental reversal cannot permanently strand non-jumping characters. Both low and high paths should share the identical bottom dock and vertical side rails, with only the top horizontal height changing after docking; do not morph a rail beneath passengers mid-lap.

3. **Safe boarding requires dwell and complete rider coverage.** A160-wide cradle can physically accommodate three32-wide collision bodies, but capture and tests must demonstrate three separate safe standing slots with margins. Add predictable stops at bottom-left, vent staging and upper boarding/exit docks, not just a single bottom-left pause. Stops must lie beside a static landing at matching top height. Carry inactive heroes through every horizontal/vertical segment and corner, and keep floor/ladder support bookkeeping from removing their carrier attachment before displacement. Separate riders should not push one another off a corner or carry through a wall.

4. **Draft ascent must overshoot the300-height gallery before lateral dismount.** A field ending exactly at300 can leave Olaf's feet too low to land on the gallery beginningx1460. Permit feet to rise safely above300, e.g. to260 before airflow fades, then let normal overhead-shield descent and steering reach the gallery across the20-unit lip gap. Keep a visible ceiling/clearance beyond the overshoot. Verify active and inactive Olaf behavior, entering with shield already raised, raising it within the flow, lowering it, missing the dismount and re-entering. The draft must accelerate velocity, never assign a destination position. Airflow strength should follow visibly completed brake/damper movement, not merely their boolean flags.

5. **Place and prove the actual arrow line.** The spec describes the damper catch across the gap but gives no hinge/catch coordinates or projectile-height clearance. Fix the catch at the normal bow's gallery firing height, visibly attached to the damper's hinge, and guarantee the gallery edge gives a clear line with no support post, loft or decorative solid blocking it. A near sword hit should obey the same exposed-glass rule if reachable. Frayed restraint and brake pin must be separate, local, visibly connected components with different material affordances; do not turn the pin into a magical named-Erik lock.

6. **Ladder top and bottom exits need defined transition semantics.** All heroes must climb using Up/Down with accessible mobile buttons; ordinary movement must leave the ladder without snapping through the landing. The ladder at650 reaches the left gallery500 fromfloor940, with explicit top edge clearance, bottom entry and safe release on both sides. No hidden auto-jump should be necessary for Baleog/Olaf at the top. Cancelling climb during a fall must restore gravity and correct support.

## Geometry and bypass observations

With the current Erik jump velocity570 and gravity1250, ideal apex rise is about130 units. The proposed110 rise and80 horizontal gap is plausible but close enough to require a real swept-collision route at60/120Hz. The time above a110-rise surface is approximately0.28–0.63 seconds; full-speed230 horizontal travel in that interval is64–146 units. A run-up can cover the stated gap, whereas a jump from rest at the exact lip may be less forgiving. Keep useful run-up length and make the landing top unambiguous. Do not lower the loft merely to rescue a failed automated route before checking actual acceleration/input timing.

Shield support is80 units above Olaf's floor under current geometry (hero44 + upTop36). A grounded shield boost alone does not reach the110 loft for non-jumping heroes. Erik can legitimately use it to simplify his jump. Such physical cooperation is welcome; do not forbid it with hero-ID checks. Likewise, an expert jump/shield route that reaches a general handle is acceptable unless it bypasses the entire mechanical chain; prove the high300 gallery still requires draft/transport before changing geometry arbitrarily.

The rectangular carrier must not visually drill through the static left gallery or right exit deck while transporting riders. One-way landing surfaces can let an empty carrier pass below a deck, but riders arriving should be deposited on its supported top without clipping. Draw all rail corners and realistic overhead/floor anchors. Background art cannot imply invisible platforms, moving floor attachments or miniature humans.

### Blocking findings

**C1 — passing cradle steals static-deck occupants.** `advanceCycleCarrier` translates any hero overlapping its old top at matching foot height, regardless of their authoritative support. A stationary hero on the300 selector gallery is therefore swept away when the high cradle passes beneath the static platform; a hero on a940 staging/right floor can likewise be dragged by the bottom pass. The current route's Olaf reunion relies on this implicit pickup rather than explicit boarding. Require genuine previous carrier support or an equivalent unambiguous support-ownership test; retain static-deck support when surfaces overlap. Add a stationary-gallery regression, actual walking boarding from a clear edge, all-three riders through corners, and selector reversals with a teammate waiting above. Static decks must never become conveyors just because the cradle shares their height.

**C2 — shield jump can bypass the entire wind chain.** The low cradle500 is only200 units below the selector gallery300. Olaf's globally valid shield shelf is80 units above his floor and Erik's current jump apex is approximately130, allowing about210 total rise. With the cradle passing beneath/near the gallery, a shield-assisted jump can physically reach the general selector without releasing either brake or damper. Preserve the global cooperative move but revise geometry to exceed that combined reach (e.g. gallery280/220rise, with draft overshoot adjusted and all route/deck art kept coherent), or independently prove collision/transport geometry makes this route impossible. Do not solve this by a named-Olaf check or a flag-gated handle that contradicts visible reachability.

**C3 — wind brake lacks continuous own-pixel motion and visible transmission.** The brake uses instantaneous atlas12→14, omitting intermediate13; `machine.brake` starts immediately and there is no easing state for the pin/rod, while damper and selector have explicit continuous poses. Render a generated intermediate sequence with a stable registered base and connect the withdrawn pin visibly to the local vent transmission. Effective airflow must await physical release completion and damper completion. Capture initial/intermediate/completed own pixels, without relying on text announcing withdrawal.
