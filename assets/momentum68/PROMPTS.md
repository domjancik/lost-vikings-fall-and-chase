# Imagegen brainstorm

Brainstorm an intricate but playable cooperative 2D side-view puzzle-platformer level, for three ADULT human-sized Vikings. Produce a beautiful annotated game-design concept sketch titled THE MOMENTUM WORKS, one continuous large chamber inside an arcade machine. Two connected acts rather than a grab-bag. MAIN LOWER ACT: Olaf braces a heavy bronze-and-ceramic rolling sphere out of an elevated cradle on the LEFT. It rolls DOWN a visible curved rail to a central fork. Baleog waits on a safely grounded sword-striker platform: his blade strikes a LARGE obvious external steering paddle attached to the sphere, turning its guide fin so the sphere follows the rising rail instead of the lower catcher. Show the physical steering paddle/joint and continuous alternate trajectories. Erik waits on a boarding ledge, jumps onto the rolling sphere, balances near its crown as it climbs, then jumps to a high ledge on RIGHT. Draw all three roles at different positions, correct human scale, long legs, no dwarves. No teleporting, no arbitrary magic switches. Failure sends the sphere into a safe lower catcher; a visible motorized return lift recirculates it to the cradle, all paths continuously connected. UPPER ACT: short challenging platforming lane for Erik with 4 visibly cracked ceramic floor tiles that sag then fall, a clearly telegraphed timed vertical laser, fireballs emitted from a mechanical furnace mouth, and a cushioned wall bumper that visibly rebounds projectiles. Give the player safe staging pads before hazard windows. At the far end a clearly connected control opens a permanent crew service lift and safe reunion deck, so nobody must remain behind. Exit above right. Strong readable path, hard but fair timing, recovery walkways and ladders for non-jumping Vikings. Pixel-art inspired rich material lighting, deep indigo industrial cavern, warm amber glass orb, dull iron rails, ivory ceramic and restrained coral hazard light. The sketch may be high-resolution illustrative design, not a runtime sprite atlas. Annotate arrows and state thumbnails showing push → steer → board/balance → leap, crumble intact/cracked/falling, laser charging/live/off. Avoid ambiguous floating supports, detached backgrounds, purely abstract overlay wires and narrow unusable ladders. Show one coherent side-view spatial layout with generous traversable gaps; upper-right hazard lane should be distinct from ball trajectory.
