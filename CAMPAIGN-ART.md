# Campaign 46 art provenance

Fresh scenery and sprites generated with the built-in imagegen tool. Project assets and originals are in `assets/campaign46/` and `assets/campaign46/source/`. Existing Arcade art is not used by the new Story renderer. A second built-in extraction retained the machine sheet and returned genuine alpha; final binary alpha uses the pipeline threshold. Background scenes retain their native 768×512 source pixels without recoloring or forced packing. Sprite cells use corrected positive-weight normalization at 96×128 and stable enemy foot pivots.

Reproduce preparation with `tools/pixel-fixer-venv/Scripts/python.exe tools/prepare-campaign46-art.py`; validate with `tools/pixel-fixer-venv/Scripts/python.exe tools/validate-campaign46-art.py`. Originals are versioned, so preparation needs no image service. For a fresh checkout, initialize the pixel-art-fixer submodule and apply `tools/pixel-fixer-positive-weights.patch` before preparing assets.

## Display-grid correction

native47 keeps all original scene PNGs unchanged. The renderer prepares a cached display grid directly from those pixels using the actual viewport and positive source-cell weights. There is no256×256 scenery limit and no stepped-resolution magnification. Original sources still support full-resolution narrative-window crops.
