# First-game Olaf reference authority

**The Lost Vikings, original first game, SNES (1992/1993-era original game).**
This is not The Lost Vikings2 and not Heroes of the Storm.

Original sprite sheet:
https://www.spriters-resource.com/snes/lostvikings/asset/8335/

Retained unmodified image: `olaf-original-snes.png` (638x620 pixels).
The source sheet credits ripping to Dazz and identifies Olaf's original SNES
animations. It was visually inspected in the normal browser and again from the
retained source file. The public page's image download supplied the original
PNG, rather than a screenshot or a resized search thumbnail.

Observed first-game traits used as proportion reference: a very broad convex
belly and back, thick arms, stout neck, substantial booted legs, green clothing,
yellow beard, horned helmet and round shield. The80 guides retain their own
adult human height/rig and rebuild those stout mass relationships at native
resolution. No source pixels were copied into the new guides.

The Sega manual and mixed-version Blizzard article were mentioned during
research but are not the final visual authority for this packet. Only the
explicitly retained original first-game SNES sheet informs this correction.
