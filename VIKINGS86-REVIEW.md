Inspected assets/warden86/concept-raw.png, actual Inside the Clockwork capture
output/playwright/clockwork71-start.png and native85 actual bound-front view.
The user's style objection is valid:85's sound articulated motion does not
resolve its narrow strict-profile torsos, bright simplified steel caps and

Warden concept establishes adult heft, visible nearer shoulder/chest/hip depth,
rounded limb volume and earthy sculpted metal/leather/flesh direction. It is

detail cannot be reduced into production art to satisfy this task.

Baleog and round barrel-bellied Olaf. Native material depth must remain readable
at actual size, with connected coarse highlight/midtone/shadow clusters rather
than noisy texture or bright outlines. The complete action kit must preserve
eight meaningful leg exchanges, torso response, stable anchors, jaw-connected
beards, low Olaf helmet, rear ladder views and shield support/grip dimensions.

## Imagegen86 style study — concept direction accepted; production source REJECTED

Read ART-DIRECTION-CLOCKWORK.md and inspected
assets/vikings86/style-study-raw.png at original resolution. Broad shoulder/front
chest planes, receding far hips and limbs, domed brow-shadow helmets, warm flesh
against recessed leather/steel and distinct adult mass are useful direction.
Olaf specifically has a protruding exposed round belly bounded by open straps,
dark lower-belly turning shadow, thick near arm and stout thighs/boots. Filling
that area with a flat olive vest would lose the study's central shape result.
Baleog's overlapping near pauldron and wraparound belt turn his broad chest;
Erik's athletic upper arm/chest and eager lean distinguish him from the others.

This is not accepted production sprite art. Bodies occupy hundreds of source
pixels and contain tiny hair strands, buckle detail, edge highlights and mottled
surface shading far beyond the intended36/38/36native standing budget. The
visually rich enlargement does not prove native density. Downsampling, grid
recovery or detail quantization would not correct that raw source failure.
**Study production native-density source gate: REJECTED; concept only.**

Native86 authorship should express those masses with deliberate connected
coarse planes, preserving shoulder/hip depth and material separation at game
size. Source acceptance remains pending the new raw native rendition; runtime
acceptance remains pending actual86 gameplay evidence.

Inspected assets/vikings86/olaf-density-study-raw.png independently. The second

**Second Olaf study production source gate: REJECTED; concept only.** No

## First direct native86 full kit — density passes; style/shape gate FAILED

Inspected contactboard-native.png at512×192, nearest4x contactboard and all
32/38/60 full frame keys in assets/vikings86-native. Read
tools/native-vikings86.py. Its64×64cells are direct native raster authorship,
not imagegen reduction; density is appropriately coarse. Retained motion shows
distinct whole-body leg phases and recognizable rear/action/equipment states.

However this initial rendition does not meet the requested visual correction.
Olaf's exposed broader belly is a useful change, but Erik still reads as a
narrow straight-sided orange torso with profile hips. Baleog still has a slablike
navy torso and a pale shoulder patch, with little readable front chest. Merely
moving shoulder chains and adding planes has not made three-quarter weight
visible at native size. Helmets read as pale horizontal caps with thin horns;
limb and boot volumes remain sparse strips. Overall it remains close to85's
diagrammatic look rather than the sculpted Clockwork/Warden material direction.

Required revision: visible chest/front width plus receding side, near/far
shoulder/hip separation, wraparound belt/waist and shaped rounded material
shadows. Preserve Olaf's belly, emphasize thick near arm/stout thighs, and
resolve Erik's rear14–17 red central head/back mass so it cannot read as a beard
facing the viewer. Rear beard early-return alone does not prove the rendered
rear appearance is correct, especially when ORIGINAL_HEAD is still called.

**First direct-native86 source density: ACCEPTED. Overall source/material/shape
gate: FAILED; runtime integration NOT APPROVED.** These judgments are independent:
binary alpha, direct native authorship and sound gait do not waive the style
failure. Runtime gate remains pending revised accepted source and actual evidence.
