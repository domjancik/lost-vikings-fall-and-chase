# Cockroach attack, turning and surface traversal

Contact damage remains. The deliberate attack is separate: commit facing during
a0.7second warning crouch, animate a0.2second native-shell lunge with damaging
reach110world units during its extended portion, then recover for0.5seconds.
The victim may evade or block with Olaf's front shield. Use the normal health
immunity, death and hazard-disable rules. Light prevents a new attack; mild flame
flicker cannot cancel a committed windup every other frame.

Patrol turns are conditional on heading outward at the endpoint. A creature
already beyond the threshold must continue inward rather than reverse every
frame. Distance drives the retained tripod gait in either direction.

Use the actual inspection shutter as a climbable wall, with continuous60world
unit/second side/top movement and quarter turns. An unobstructed wall permits
crossing; the actual neighboring service deck prevents descent on its crowded
side, so the creature turns on top and returns down the exposed side. Opening
the shutter releases it under gravity. Rotate body contact/arrow bounds and
cast visibility from the body center in exposed air. A killed climber falls away
and settles on real support rather than preserving an elevated corpse floor.
