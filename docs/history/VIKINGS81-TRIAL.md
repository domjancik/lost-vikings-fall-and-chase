## Native constraints and evidence

One native pixel is two world units; each cell is64×64 native pixels and128×128 world units. The body height is36/38/36 native pixels for Erik/Baleog/Olaf, excluding helmet horns. Root32,40 and planted exclusive sole62 remain registered.

Erik's eight gait phases include contact/compression/recovery and two airborne phases, strong arm counter-swing, knee drive and a three-segment nine-pixel beard with trailing motion. Existing action identifier `walk` names this run packet for mapping compatibility.

Olaf's front shield is a24-pixel physical round disc yawed45° and rolled−8°, presenting an approximately17×24 face. Its grip follows the raised body; it clears the ground. Raised shield dimensions and rider plane remain fixed. Raised poses duck the head enough for horns to clear its underside.
