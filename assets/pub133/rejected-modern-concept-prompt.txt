Visual brainstorming board for a richly detailed pixel-art side-scrolling cooperative game intro level, The Neon Stag, the three adult-human Viking friends' regular arcade pub. Three wide horizontal concept panels, each a different practical layout of the same intimate rainy-night British/Nordic pub. Warm amber wood, modeled brass bevels, dark teal rain-lit windows, deep burgundy upholstery, generous layered depth, connected human-scale architecture. This is a concept exploration, not a production sprite sheet. Keep characters at believable adult human scale, not dwarves, furniture not giant.
Scene ideas to explore: a cheerful chubby bearded horned-helmet Olaf carrying four frothing tankards on a horizontally raised round shield; a wheeled loaded keg trolley he can push out of the service aisle using shield held forward; dynamic red-bearded Erik jumping to a high service shelf from a real stool/counter, optionally from Olaf's shield; armored Baleog at a safe wooden pub target range, a clearly exposed soft rope holding its rolled target cover to demonstrate a sword cut, then a bow/dart shot at a physical target; many distinct regular patrons chatting, drinking, a fiddler, bartender greeting the crew, a dog by the hearth. Independent pockets of activity, tables and service routes, no character painted into platforms.
At far end of each layout a small intriguing newly delivered brass-and-glass cabinet with an impossible glowing object/clockwork seed, near a creaking water-stained boarded cellar hatch. All three gather to look; the damaged timbers crack and tilt, then the trio fall into the sewer beneath. Show this as a small end vignette/cutaway connected to the pub. The consequence must be physical and legible. Avoid portals, lasers, epic combat, pipes as arbitrary puzzles, arbitrary colored switches, UI/web panels, illegible generated writing. Give the space a welcoming lived-in identity and a latent uneasy clue, not a generic fantasy tavern. Detailed dimensional pixel clusters, strong material texture and rim light, comparable richness to a premium16/32bit clockwork adventure, no smooth vector/cartoon flattening. Distinct silhouettes and warm human relationships. The target game should be protected from patrons' traffic and the final object visibly draws all three friends' attention. Prioritize usable side-view level geometry and a few recoverable playful jumps over a collage of decorations.
