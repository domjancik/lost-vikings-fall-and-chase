## Provisional concepts

1. **Storm Loom:** strongest novel spatial verb if shuttle travel actually weaves a supported walkable route. Olaf's shield must carry or redirect a physical moving load; Erik reaches a separated service point; Baleog cuts an authored weak textile restraint, visibly changing tension. Avoid a timer toggling arbitrary platforms. Thread and spool endpoints require supports, visible tension and a safe failed-cycle return.
2. **Counterweight Chapel:** promising traceable balance machinery but vulnerable to ordinary pressure plates with new decoration. Require actual articulated load transfer, visible pivot/chain connections and shield support rather than named-hero locks. Teach balancing safely, then combine positioning, a cut restraint and an elevated access maneuver. Reversible balance must leave every hero a return path.
3. **Resonant Archive:** potentially strong worldbuilding, but pneumatic control risks hidden acoustic flags or another valve/pin sequence. Bellows, ducts and diaphragms must visibly transmit the change. Require a new physical route transformation, all three abilities and no invisible frequency/password prerequisite.

### Image-informed selection

Inspected assets/level96/concepts/concept-1.png, concept-2.png and concept-3.png with their exact prompt files. Revised recommendation: **Counterweight Chapel > Storm Loom > Resonant Archive**.

Chapel communicates the best single shared physical machine: visible fulcrum, suspended loads, changing beam height, upper ratchet and height-aligned ranged target. Its lower explanatory panels show a plausible path from experimentation to concurrent cooperation to a retained level beam. This is a conceptual design pass, conditional on hero position/weight genuinely affecting the beam, moving collision planes matching its slope and a persistent physical latch permitting reunion. Merely replacing the beam with two animated lift pads would discard the selected novelty. Baleog's target needs an authored exposed weak latch, and Erik must reach the ratchet through actual jump geometry. A recovery floor must connect to both arms without bypassing the solve.

Storm has the richest weather/world atmosphere but weaker causal continuity. Several rope/shuttle endpoints are unexplained; threading, redirected wind and shutter completion are named rather than physically specified. It remains a good future design if shuttle travel really transports material through visibly tensioned ports and the installed fabric changes a supported route. Do not select it merely for its attractive panorama.

Archive clearly diagrams exhaust→bellows→resonator→lift linkage, but Olaf holding pressure and all three riding the resulting lift conflict without a visible pressure-retaining latch. Grille shots repeat an existing timing verb, and the resonant spring still needs an intelligible release target. It ranks third for mechanical novelty.

All three concepts use high-detail presentation art and are retained as composition/story evidence only. They are not production sprite source acceptance. Large inscriptions and industrial machinery are acceptable setting cues; final inhabited doors, handles and stairs still require adult scale evidence.

6. Prefer two distinct eyelets: a safe moving-span pass and a constrained timed-clutch pass. A third repeated eyelet adds duration more readily than a new challenge.
