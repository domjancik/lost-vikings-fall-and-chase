# Terrain material sets — terrain48

Both Story and Arcade terrain use a shared eight-material atlas with eight roles: top-left, top repeat, top-right, left side, quiet infill A/B, right side, and bottom edge. Only the walking boundary gets a lit bevel. Interior texture is subdued over an opaque material mass; its alternate variant is sparse and deterministic. Sprites are clipped to each floor/platform's collision rectangle, including partial cells and narrow landings. The ground and heroes share the same top coordinate. Existing platform supports remain attached.

The generated source is versioned at assets/terrain48/source.png; the runtime atlas is assets/terrain48/terrain.png. Preparation uses tools/prepare-terrain48.py, explicit measured source rectangles (the generated source has10 columns and a taller final row), and the corrected positive-weight pixel fixer. Each of64 pieces is checked against its source-cell color bounds. The256×256 atlas holds32×32 tiles, which render at64 world units; this is a sprite sheet, not a full-scene background resolution policy.

Regression tests check one surface boundary, infill-only interior, side/bottom roles, partial-platform clipping, deterministic variations and exact2-world-unit sprite pixels. Browser survey captures all19 levels and mobile. Mechanics/collision geometry are unchanged.

## Imagegen prompt

Create a production 2D side-view platform-game TERRAIN TILESET as one square image, exact 8 columns by 8 rows, evenly spaced square tiles filling the entire sheet edge to edge, no gaps, margins, labels, letters, HUD or objects. Flat orthographic side view, deliberate crisp 1990s pixel art, restrained dark material colors, NO glowing accents, screens, panels, coins, gems, windows, logos, medallions or repeated conspicuous motifs. The sheet is an engineering tile atlas, not a scenic illustration. Every row is one matched seamless material set. The 8 tile types left to right: top-left solid corner; top-center repeatable walking surface with a narrow 6-pixel lit bevel at its very top; top-right solid corner; left vertical side edge on a quiet wall face; seamless dark infill A; seamless dark infill B with a few tiny subtle irregularities; right vertical side edge on a quiet wall face; bottom-edge with a thin dark underside. Full tiles are opaque solid material; no transparent holes. Center infill has NO borders, top/bottom ridges, bolts or outlines, very low contrast, sparse texture. All tile edge colors within each row must match exactly so infill joins invisibly and top-center repeats invisibly; corners and side edges use only thin shading at outer edge. Top-edge tiles are mostly quiet wall with a narrow readable walking ledge along the top, no repeating rectangular framing. Eight rows top to bottom: 1 aged dark brass plates, desaturated ochre with soft tarnish; 2 dark blue enamel metal, subdued coral traces only extremely sparse; 3 indigo slate with ivory dust small restrained flecks; 4 muted sage ceramic and terracotta seams sparse; 5 deep aubergine copper-woven material, no shiny accents; 6 blue-gray structural stone/metal, midnight cyan very muted; 7 matte charcoal basalt with subtle bronze grit; 8 dark warm wood and paper-fiber structural material, minimal grain. The set should form large coherent ground masses below heroes, rather than repeating decorated blocks. Pixel clusters and consistent tile grid. Avoid photographic/painterly textures, gradients, checkerboards, thick tile borders and strong repeating ornament.


## Refinement

The initial generated source is retained at assets/terrain48/source-initial.png. The selected source.png is the imagegen refinement: preserve all ten column/eight row bounds and material palettes; remove large cracks, diagonal scratches, framed panels, seams, vertical grooves and conspicuous repeating shapes from wall interiors; infill columns contain only near-flat dark material with tiny faint pixel flecks, matching edge colors and no trim; wood grain is subtle and short. Corner/outer edge bevels remain readable. The runtime crop excludes all separator pixels, and a regression checks infill edge darkness and low contrast. Moving platform visuals snap to the two-unit grid without modifying simulation coordinates.
