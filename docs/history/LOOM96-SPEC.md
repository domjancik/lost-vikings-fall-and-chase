# The Storm Loom: a weather machine in the deeper arcade

The machine repairs the sky it projects into the next world. A torn pattern has
left its exit shutter mechanically disconnected. The crew restores its thread,
then the repaired winding drum raises the shutter and retains it for reunion.
This adds a seventh Machine Rooms level, without replacing the existing campaigns.

Three imagegen concepts retained in assets/level96/concepts: Counterweight Chapel,
Storm Loom, Resonant Archive. Chapel has clearest shared load physics; Archive
has a readable pneumatic chain but overlaps prior valve/gear rooms. Storm's new
spatial verb—carrying a bobbin through physical guide apertures—wins once its
material/pressure causality and recovery are made explicit. Critique is independent.

The pump is positive-displacement and driven by a visible continuous shaft. Olaf's
front shield physically intercepts its open relief exhaust. Actual shield segment
collision, not a station or character-ready flag, raises backpressure/reservoir
level. A connected piston lift carries Baleog to a firing gallery. Pressure changes
smoothly; piston/shuttles accelerate and coast, never snap when pressure changes.
Pressure gauge, pistons and plumbing show the same state.

Baleog shoots an exposed arrow latch from the gallery to release the docked bobbin.
The target visibly rotates its mechanical pawl. Olaf supplies a raised shield
step on the human-scale service deck: only Erik's actual jump reaches the higher
bobbin deck from that walking plane. All stations may be used by any hero; no ID
locks are invented. A safe dock teaches this without damage.

Erik carries the bobbin over continuously moving shuttles. Thread follows his hand
and its retained physical route. It must cross two genuine guide apertures in
order, tested against swept hand position through the aperture plane. First guide
teaches a moving-span jump; second adds a vertically moving plate, a timed clutch
grate and warned loom beaters. Baleog's second clearly mounted target opens the
clutch for a generous switching/travel interval and is reusable. Olaf maintains
pressure while the other two execute the traverse; the first safe pause deck
allows switching without being dragged into a hazard.

The final bobbin dock winds material continuously into a visible cloth drum;
connected transmission lifts the exit shutter. A ratchet retains the completed
door independently of pressure, so nobody must stay behind. All crew exit together
on the lower floor. Misses fall onto a continuous recovery floor. Carried material
stays with its holder and completed guides retain progress; any reeling/reset must
move the bobbin/thread continuously. Fatal hazard damage uses normal retry.
