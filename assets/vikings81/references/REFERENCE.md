# Original first-game authority

`olaf-original-snes.png` is the unchanged638x620 Olaf sprite sheet from the
first **The Lost Vikings** SNES game, credited on the sheet to ripper Dazz:
https://www.spriters-resource.com/snes/lostvikings/asset/8335/

The original reference was visually inspected and retained in vikings80, then
copied unchanged here. Use its stout belly/back, round oblique shield, low
helmet and yellow beard. Do not substitute Lost Vikings2 or Heroes of the Storm.
The procedural81 pixels do not copy source sprite pixels.

The newly authored orientation/costume authority is
`../olaf-shield-helmet-review.png`: native45-degree shield yaw, slight roll,
covered eye, unchanged round diameter and fixed overhead rider plane.
