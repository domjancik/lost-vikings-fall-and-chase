A LITTLE TEAMWORK

Leave no Viking behind.

Switch between your three heroes. Only the selected hero moves; the others stay where you leave them.

Erik jumps with Space / F / Enter and performs a head bash with D / Alt after a running start. Baleog swings his sword with Space / F / Enter (Action) and shoots with D (Alt). Olaf always holds his shield. D / Alt puts it in front; Space / Action raises it overhead. Repeated presses keep that pose. Erik can stand on his raised shield; it also blocks falling hazards.

Explore the overhead shelves and repair the fuse panel with E / S. Olaf faces right and raises his front shield in the arcade firing lane to open the rogue arcade’s armor. Baleog shoots from behind him. Bring all three to the unlocked exit.

Arcade worlds: cargo lifts carry the whole crew; Olaf’s front shield reflects arrows and fire. Haunted doors use E / S / USE to travel. Olaf can push heavy machinery while his front shield is raised. Erik run-bashes drums and bellows by building speed before pressing D.

Inventory: USE / E picks up nearby items. BAG / I opens four slots for the selected Viking. Choose a slot, use Q or USE ITEM, give it to a nearby Viking, or drop it to recover later. Apples heal one heart; meat heals two. Bombs have a two-second fuse and hurt Vikings inside the blast.

Water: Erik swims with the direction controls. Olaf floats and pushes the floating compressor with his front shield. Baleog sinks. Air dots show remaining breath; open-bottom bells refill air. USE grips a crank; RIGHT winds and USE releases.

Ladders: Up/Down climbs with any Viking; move sideways to step off. Olaf’s overhead shield catches an upward draft. Outside a draft it slows his fall.

Movement accelerates and brakes. Erik runs fastest; Olaf is heaviest. V or the zoom button cycles 1×, 1.5× and 2× views. R restarts. Escape pauses. Hazards deal damage. Zero health or falling into a void kills a Viking: R retries the level. All three must survive.
