# Drowned Regulator correction

Replace the polished toy-like gold/teal machinery with coherent worn foundry
materials: dark iron, aged bronze, restrained copper edges, readable rivets,
recessed mechanisms, exposed hoses and large intentional corrosion patches.
The original Warden86 concept is the material benchmark. Preserve the existing
puzzle, registrations, air-bell cavities, mounting points and human scale.
Mechanical construction and material fidelity are review requirements, not
merely correct atlas dimensions. Avoid mottled paint, soft gradients, shiny
enamel and scattered gold highlights.

## Water and mechanics

Replace the dashed surface and rectangular jet with layered native-pixel
ripples, irregular moving reflections, submerged caustic clusters, rising
bubbles, drainage flow and restrained refracted light. Render detail positions
on the2world-unit pixel grid; simulation movement remains continuous.
Air-bell cavities must read as breathable clear air, with visible curved
menisci, water-edge shading and a distinct waterline. Do not fill the cavities
or disguise their physical boundaries with a generic cyan rectangle.

Olaf's shield intercepts the actual jet progressively as it overlaps the
stream. Use continuous overlap plus a short damped response, rather than a
hard x/y location switch. Upstream water remains active; downstream flow and
force attenuate consistently. Buoyant side entry and settling must remain
velocity-driven with bounded acceleration and no surface-height teleport.
Existing underwater roles, air consumption, crank/sluice states and normal
controls remain causal and recoverable.
